I am a theoretical quantum chemist and computational physicist specializing in **non-equilibrium phase-space kinetics**, **strongly correlated electron dynamics**, and **continuous-variable quantum computation**. 

As the Founder & CEO of [**Point Reyes Sound, Inc.**](https://pointreyessound.com), I develop mathematical methods and scalable solvers that replace factorial multireference active spaces with deterministic, polynomial $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ Quantum Boltzmann Equation (QBE-SCF) phase-space propagation.

## Research Programs

### 1. The Quantum Boltzmann Equation for Electronic Structure
Propagating the one-electron reduced density matrix through non-equilibrium Bhatnagar-Gross-Krook phase-space relaxation, eliminating unphysical symmetry breaking and regularizing mean-field singularities at polynomial $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ computational cost.

### 2. Generalized Pauli Conditions & Reduced Density Matrices
Formulating mathematical constraints on the eigenvalues of one-particle reduced density matrices ($N$-representability polytope pinning) to prove that boundary saturation enforces structural sparsity in CI wavefunctions and dictates dynamic state transitions.

### 3. Electronic Structure of Energy Materials & MOFs
First-principles simulation and energy decomposition analysis of open-shell transition metal centers in metal-organic frameworks for ambient-temperature hydrogen storage and selective reversible oxygen chemisorption.

### 4. Frontier Computational Chemistry Software
Co-development of the Q-Chem 5 package, implementing open-shell electronic structure algorithms, density matrix methods, and automated active-space partitioning.
