## Biography & Scientific Lineage

I am a theoretical quantum chemist and computational physicist specializing in **non-equilibrium phase-space kinetics**, **strongly correlated electron dynamics**, and **continuous-variable quantum computation**. 

As the Founder & CEO of [**Point Reyes Sound, Inc.**](https://pointreyessound.com), I develop mathematical methods and scalable solvers that replace factorial multireference active spaces with deterministic, polynomial $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ Quantum Boltzmann Equation (QBE-SCF) phase-space propagation.

- **Founder & CEO**, Point Reyes Sound, Inc. (2025–Present)
- **Quantum Software Engineer**, PsiQuantum (2025)
- **Postdoctoral Researcher**, UC Berkeley & Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (2023–2024, Head-Gordon & Long groups)
- **Ph.D. in Chemistry / Chemical Physics**, The University of Chicago (2017, Mazziotti group)
- **B.Tech. in Chemical Engineering**, Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (2012)

## Flagship Research Programs

### 1. Continuous Phase-Space Kinetics & QBE-SCF
Developing the **Quantum Boltzmann Equation Self-Consistent Field (QBE-SCF)** solver, propagating the one-electron reduced density matrix (1-RDM) through non-equilibrium Bhatnagar-Gross-Krook (BGK) collision relaxation. Eliminates unphysical spin-symmetry breaking and smoothly regularizes mean-field singularities near conical intersections at polynomial $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ cost.
* **Preprint:** [arXiv:2608.14979](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14979) `[physics.chem-ph, quant-ph]`
* **Open Benchmark Suite:** [`point-reyes-sound/p1_qbescf`](https://github.com/point-reyes-sound/p1_qbescf) *(Reproduction data; core solver engine is proprietary commercial IP)*
* **Interactive 3D Engine:** [`point-reyes-sound/point-reyes-sound`](https://github.com/point-reyes-sound/point-reyes-sound)

### 2. Generalized Pauli Conditions & Reduced Density Matrices
Formulating mathematical constraints on the eigenvalues of one-particle reduced density matrices ($N$-representability polytope pinning). Proved that saturation of generalized Pauli boundary conditions (Borland-Dennis and Klyachko conditions) enforces structural sparsity in CI wavefunctions and dictates dynamic state transitions in atoms, molecules, and open quantum systems.
* **Key Paper:** *Phys. Rev. A* **89**, 042505 (2014) [DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevA.89.042505](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.89.042505)

### 3. Electronic Structure of Energy Materials & MOFs
First-principles simulations and energy decomposition analysis (ALMO-EDA) of open-shell transition metal centers in Metal-Organic Frameworks (MOFs) for ambient-temperature hydrogen ($H_2$) storage and selective reversible oxygen chemisorption.
* **Key Paper:** *Phys. Chem. Chem. Phys.* **26**, 7356 (2024) [DOI: 10.1039/D3CP05540J](https://doi.org/10.1039/D3CP05540J) *(PCCP Hot Article)*

### 4. Frontier Computational Chemistry Software
Co-developer of the Q-Chem 5 package, implementing open-shell electronic structure algorithms, density matrix methods, and automated active-space partitioning.
* **Key Paper:** *J. Chem. Phys.* **155**, 084801 (2021) [DOI: 10.1063/5.0055522](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0055522)
